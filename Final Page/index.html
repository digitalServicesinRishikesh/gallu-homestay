Namaste and a warm welcome to The Grand Haveli – your serene escape nestled in the foothills of the majestic Himalayas and alongside the sacred Huel River. We are honored to have you with us and hope that your stay will be a blend of relaxation, rejuvenation, and unforgettable experiences. Our resort is designed to offer you a peaceful retreat from the hustle of daily life, where modern comforts meet the tranquility of nature. Whether you’re here to indulge in our luxurious facilities, with savor the flavors at our multi-cuisine restaurant, as well as enjoy the serenity of our outdoor pool, or explore the spiritual and adventure-filled offerings of Rishikesh – we aim to make your stay truly special. Let us know if you need any assistance during your time here, whether it's arranging a yoga session, booking a sightseeing tour, or simply guiding you to the best spots around the resort. Your comfort and happiness are our priority.

Step into our elegantly designed Deluxe Premium Rooms, where comfort and luxury seamlessly blend. Enjoy spacious living areas, rich wooden flooring, and large windows that fill the room with natural light. Every detail is crafted to exceed your expectations, ensuring an unforgettable experience.

Once again, thank you for choosing The Grand Haveli. We hope you have a memorable and enriching experience.

Imagine a serene, tranquil escape nestled within the heart of nature, where the sound of rustling leaves, the song of birds, and the gentle murmur of a nearby stream are the only things that greet your senses. This resort, surrounded by the raw beauty of nature, offers more than just a place to stay—it's a sanctuary for the soul. This resort is perfectly situated in an area where lush forests, sprawling meadows, and majestic mountain ranges come together in harmony. It could be perched on a hillside with panoramic views of a nearby huel river, or perhaps tucked within a valley where the land is blanketed in vibrant wildflowers.

The environment is unspoiled, with no rush of city life— just an immersive natural beauty that calms the mind. The resort offers a wide range of activities that allow guests to reconnect with nature while staying active and engaged. Whether you're seeking adventure or relaxation, there's something for everyone.
