Our Ghallu Homestay is designed to offer you a peaceful retreat from the hustle of daily life, where modern comforts meet the tranquility of nature. A Personal Welcome From Ghallu Homestay

At the heart of Ghallu Homestay lies the genuine warmth and nurturing spirit of your host, Mrs. Rupa Devi. As the wife of Mr. Rajendra Singh Pundir, Mrs. Rupa Devi is the soul of this mountain home. A pillar of the local Ghughtiyani Talli community, she brings an authentic, traditional touch to your stay that you simply won't find in a hotel. When you arrive at Ghallu Homestay, you aren't just a guest—you are welcomed as a member of the family. Mrs. Rupa Devi takes immense pride in her role as a host, ensuring that every visitor feels right at home from the moment they step through the door. Our hospitality is defined by: Authentic Himalayan Care: Experience the heartfelt, nurturing nature of a local village mother who prioritizes your comfort and peace above all else. Choosing to stay with Mrs. Rupa Devi and the Pundir family means trading impersonal service for a genuine, life-enriching connection. It is the perfect opportunity to slow down, disconnect from the digital world, and reconnect with the simple, restorative joys of village life under the watchful, caring eye of your dedicated host.
